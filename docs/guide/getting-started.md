# Getting started

## Opening it

Go to the app and press **Start free**. There is no account, no email and no
card. If you would rather have your name and picture on your profile you can
sign in with Google, but every single feature works identically without it.

## The six questions

Setup asks for:

| | Why it is asked |
|---|---|
| **Sex, age, height, weight** | These four decide your calorie targets and your starting weights. They are the only reason they are asked. |
| **Your goal** | Picks the training block you open on — muscle, fat loss, strength, athleticism, or all of it slowly. |
| **Experience** | Scales both how much volume you get and how heavy FEROX opens you. Be honest; it will correct itself either way. |
| **Days a week** | Decides the shape of your week. Two days is a full-body split, six is push/pull/legs twice. |
| **Equipment** | Full gym, home setup, a pair of dumbbells, or nothing at all. FEROX will never programme something you cannot do. |
| **Anything that hurts** | Knees, shoulders, lower back, wrists, hips. Exercises that load a joint you flagged are removed entirely. |

It takes about thirty seconds, and at the end you have a real plan rather than
an empty app.

## What you get

Open **Workouts → Your plan** and there is a full week: which days, which
exercises, how many sets, how many reps, how long to rest, and **what weight to
put on the bar**. You have not logged anything yet — all of that is worked out
from what you just told it.

Press **Start this session** on any day and the logger opens with everything
pre-filled. Change what you actually did, save, and that is the whole loop.

## What to do on day one

Nothing clever. Run the session as written. The weights will probably feel
light, and that is deliberate — see [What weight to lift](weights.md). If a set
was easy, put the real number in when you log it, and FEROX will believe you.

## If you are coming back after a break

Profile → **Start over** clears every stat, medal and meal and restarts the plan
clock, while keeping your measurements and your training year. It asks you to
type a phrase first, because there is no undo.
