# Themes and secrets

## Dark and light

The moon/sun button in the sidebar. That one is not a secret.

Your mode is remembered per device, since which one you want depends on where
you are rather than who you are.

## There is more in here than that

FEROX ships with four colour palettes. You can see one of them.

The others are **Neon Wolf** (hot pink and neon blue), **Cosmic** (deep violet
with starlight) and **Nautical** (deep water, teal and brass). Each has its own
dark *and* light mode, and Ember is always there to switch back to.

They are locked. The app will not tell you how to unlock them — that is rather
the point of an easter egg — but it will tell you honestly that they exist,
because a secret nobody can tell is there is just dead code.

Profile → Appearance shows how many you have found.

## What we will say

- There is a hidden prompt somewhere in the app.
- There is more than one way to open it. One of them works on a phone.
- It does not care about capitals, spaces or punctuation, so half-remembering a
  phrase from a friend is good enough.
- There are more things to find than just the themes. Most of them do nothing
  but talk back to you.

## Once unlocked

Palettes follow your account rather than your device, so signing in on a new
phone brings your colours with it. Unlocks survive a full reset — Profile →
Start over clears your stats, not the things you found.
